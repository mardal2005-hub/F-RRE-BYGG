About Førre Bygg

A building company you can build on – rooted locally in Tysvær.

Førre Bygg is based in Førresfjorden in Tysvær and works on the construction, upgrading and repair of homes, cabins and garages. We place great emphasis on good craftsmanship, orderly processes and close customer contact throughout the entire project.

We carry out carpentry work both on an hourly basis and at a fixed price, and can bring in skilled subcontractors across all trades. That way we take responsibility for the whole project – and make sure you know what's happening along the way, from start to finished result.
