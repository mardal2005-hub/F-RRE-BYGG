New builds

Construction of new homes, cabins and buildings – planned and carried out with precision.

Building something new? We take care of the carpentry from foundation to finished building, and coordinate the other trades along the way.

Førre Bygg carries out the construction of homes, cabins and other buildings. We place emphasis on good planning, orderly execution and solid craftsmanship at every stage – so the result lasts and matches what we agreed.

We do the carpentry on an hourly basis or at a fixed price, whichever suits the project best. Where needed, we bring in skilled subcontractors across all trades, so you have one fixed point of contact all the way.

What we can help with

  • Construction of homes, cabins and buildings
  • Carpentry by the hour or at a fixed price
  • Coordination of subcontractors (plumbing, electrical, masonry, etc.)
  • Close follow-up from start to completion
